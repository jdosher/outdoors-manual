    Edmonds Fishing Pier

    The Edmonds pier is the gold-standard public pier in central Puget Sound — free access, lit at night, ADA-compliant, rain-covered sections, a fish-cleaning station, restrooms, and parking essentially on the structure. It sits in Marine Area 9 (Admiralty Inlet) and the pier itself has its own salmon season that's more generous than the surrounding waters. Lingcod and salmon are the marquee finfish, but the pier's real cult following is for winter night squid jigging under the bright pole lights — a uniquely accessible Puget Sound experience. Tide is everything here; high slack and the flood that follows are typically the productive windows. Plan trips around the tide chart, not your schedule.

    Species & Regulations (2026, MA 9)

    Always verify at WDFW before fishing — these change mid-season.

    Lingcod

    • Season: May 1 – June 15 (hook & line). Spear: May 21 – June 15.
    • Size: Min 26", max 36" (slot rule — fish over 36" must be released).
    • Limit: 1 per day.
    • Tactics: Bottom-bouncing a herring or large jig at slack tide. Lings hold near pier pilings and the rocky structure below the deck.

    Salmon

    Pier has a separate, more generous season than open-water MA 9. * Pier season: Oct 1 – July 31, plus Aug 1 – Sept 30 (verify dates yearly). * Chinook: Min 22" during open periods. * Coho (Silver): Must be released in MA 9 for 2026 — including from the pier. Coho retention happens at Deception Pass (MA 8-1), not here. * Chum: Must be released. * Pink: Aug 1 – Sept 30, 2 additional pink allowed beyond standard salmon limit (pink runs are odd years; next strong run: 2027). * Tactics: Buoy 10-style float-and-herring, Buzz Bombs, casting Pt. Wilson Darts. Mornings.

    Squid (market squid, Loligo opalescens)

    • Season: Year-round, but pier fishery runs late September through December, peaking November–December.
    • License: Shellfish/seaweed license (separate from finfish).
    • Limit: 10 lbs / 5 gallons per day (verify current WDFW rule).
    • Tactics: Night fishing under the pier lights with squid jigs (2–4" weighted glow lures). Tall pole lights at Edmonds shine far out — cast 40–60 ft out and jig in. High tide on a cloudy/rainy night is peak. Bring a bucket.

    Bottomfish (flounder, sole, sculpin, greenling)

    • Limit: 15 bottomfish total per day combined.
    • Restriction: No fishing in waters deeper than 120 ft.
    • Tactics: Small hooks with sand shrimp, herring strip, or sandworm on a bottom rig. Productive year-round but best at slack and slow tide stages.

    Cabezon

    • Season: May 1 – Nov 30.
    • Size: Min 18".
    • Limit: 1 per day.
    • Notes: Ambush predator holding tight to pier structure; aggressive on jigs and bait.

    Rockfish

    • Status: CLOSED to retention in Puget Sound. Release immediately, ideally with a descender device.

    Logistics

    License WA saltwater fishing license; shellfish license for squid
    Catch Record Card Required for salmon — record immediately
