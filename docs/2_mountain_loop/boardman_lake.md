# Boardman Lake

Boardman sits in a cirque on the north slope of Bald Mountain, ringed by old-growth cedars and Pacific silver fir thick enough to swallow the basin whole. The approach is just one mile, but the density of the timber and the way the trail drops into the cirque make it feel like a real backcountry outing. The trail passes small, marshy Lake Evan a few minutes in — a decent warm-up cast if Boardman is busy. A large log jam at the outlet creek serves as the main casting platform: stable, brush-free, and right above the drop-off. Faint social paths trace the east and north shores out to a few additional spots, but bushwhacking off them gets gnarly quickly. Rainbow and brook trout are the residents and aren't picky; this is a good "low investment, real return" lake for an afternoon.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Large outlet log jam at trail's end is the prime platform. Faint social paths trace the east/north shoreline to drop-offs.
* **Approach:** Pass small marshy Lake Evan a few minutes from the trailhead before pushing on to Boardman.
