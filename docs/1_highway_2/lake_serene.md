# Lake Serene

Serene is locked into a granite cirque directly beneath the sheer 3,000-ft north face of Mt. Index — possibly the most dramatic alpine setting on Hwy 2. The trail is a workout: 3.6 miles and 2,000 ft of gain, much of it on engineered stairs and switchbacks chiseled out of the cliff bands. The lake stays cold all summer because the wall above shadows it for half the day. Shore access is largely limited to "Lunch Rock," a flat outcrop on the south shore that juts out over the drop-off and serves as the de facto fishing pier. Camping isn't allowed, so this is a day trip — start early. Fishing-wise this is a "scenic with bonus fishing" outing more than a destination angling lake; the rainbows are there but the place is really about the setting and the effort.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** "Lunch Rock" outcrop juts into deep water — natural casting platform, no back-cast obstruction. Shore access is limited; WDFW recommends a raft for serious fishing.
* **Stocking:** WDFW recently switched from aircraft to hand stocking. Best fishing is spring after stocking, holding into June.
* **Difficulty:** This is a workout. Pack light.
