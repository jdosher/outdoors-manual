# Lake Valhalla

Valhalla sits just below the crest of the Cascades near Stevens Pass, a true subalpine lake tucked under the granite shoulder of Lichtenberg Mountain. The Pacific Crest Trail traces its eastern edge, so you'll share the basin with thru-hikers in late summer. Unlike most cirque lakes, Valhalla has a long sandy beach on its northeast shore — a rarity in the Cascades and the main reason it's so shore-fishable. The water deepens fast off the beach, putting cruising trout within easy casting range without a boat. Westslope cutthroat and rainbow are both present and willing on small spinners or a bubble-and-fly rig. The Smith Brook approach is moderate; the gain is real but spread over 3.5 miles, and the basin's openness makes the final descent feel like a reward.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Long sandy beach on the northeast shore offers wide-open, brush-free casting — uncommon in steep-walled cirques.
* **Approach:** Smith Brook Trail is the standard access; PCT-from-Stevens-Pass is longer but more scenic.
