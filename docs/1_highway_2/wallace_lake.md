# Wallace Lake

Wallace sits well above the popular Wallace Falls day-hike traffic, deep enough into the timber that the basin feels genuinely backcountry despite being inside a state park. The approach uses the Greg Ball Trail — an old logging rail grade that's smooth and steady, making the 4.5 miles feel shorter than the distance suggests. The lake is unusual for the Cascades in that it holds lake trout (mackinaw) alongside rainbow and brook trout; the mackinaw run deep, so shore anglers mostly tag the rainbows and brookies. Stream inlets and outlets are the productive zones, with gravel bars and downed logs giving you stable platforms to cast from. Backcountry sites at Wallace and nearby Jay Lake mean you can stretch this into an overnight, but the permit goes through the state-park call-in number at (360) 793-0420. Open year-round and two-pole legal — a rare combination in WA.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Gravel bars and downed logs near inlet/outlet streams concentrate fish.
* **Approach:** Greg Ball Trail (old logging rail grade) is smooth and steady — long but easy underfoot.
* **Note:** Mackinaw presence makes this unusual for a hike-in lake — they hold deep, so shore fishing tends to favor the brookies and rainbows.
