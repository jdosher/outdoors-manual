# Highway 2 Corridor Overview

* **Primary Passes:** Northwest Forest Pass, Discover Pass (State Parks/WDFW lands near Gold Bar/Index).
* **Key Targets:** Native Coastal Cutthroat, stocked Rainbows, and Brook Trout in high-alpine cirques.
* **Seasonality:** Most alpine lakes ice out late May / early June. Lower-elevation lakes fishable earlier.
