# Wenas Wildlife Area

Expansive arid shrub-steppe: rolling sage hills, deep rimrock canyons, grassy flats. Good glassing terrain.

## Scouting Report
* **Terrain Style:** Steeps, rocky draws, open sage. Mid-slope transitions between water and cover hold birds.
* **Vehicle/Trailer Access:** Wenas Road gravel access is good. Dispersed pullouts accommodate trailers up to ~26 ft.

## Field Notes
* _Late season — south-facing rimrock melts off first; birds hold tight to rock for warmth._
