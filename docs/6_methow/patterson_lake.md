# Patterson Lake

Patterson is the western Methow Valley's classic year-round lake — small enough to feel intimate, productive enough to draw anglers in every season including winter ice fishery. Just west of Winthrop, set in dry pine and open meadow, it's the kind of place where the trifecta of fishing in the morning, hiking the Methow Trails network in the afternoon, and rolling out the Dobsonian after sunset works seamlessly. Unlike most WA lakes it holds a real diversity of fish — rainbow trout are the headline, but kokanee, yellow perch, and both bass species share the water. WDFW maintains a gravel boat launch on the west side; the east shore is dominated by Patterson Lake Resort with rental cabins and small boats. Discover Pass required for the WDFW access.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Shore access at the WDFW launch and along the public-access stretches. Most of the lake is fishable from float tube; some shoreline is private.
* **Fish:** Rainbow trout primary (stocked and carryover). Kokanee average 10–11". Yellow perch 6–8" — schooling, fun for kids. Bass best at dawn/dusk.
* **Ice fishery:** Increasingly popular winter target — trout, kokanee, and perch all caught through the ice.
* **Pressure:** Moderate. Patterson Lake Resort traffic adds shoreline activity but the lake itself isn't crowded.
