# Pearrygin Lake State Park

Pearrygin is the gold-standard developed-camping anchor for any Methow Valley trip — 1,186 acres of state park wrapped around 11,000 ft of shoreline on a spring-fed lake just four miles northeast of Winthrop. The park has 153 campsites across two areas (76 standard, 50 full hookup, 27 partial hookup), a boat launch, a swim beach, and 3 miles of trail. The water is genuinely good for trout (stocked rainbows averaging 10–13" with carryover fish to 15"+, plus brown trout and kokanee), and the surrounding land transitions from sage flat to ponderosa benches with sharp peaks visible to the west. Set up camp here, fish at dawn, hunt the Methow Wildlife Area in the morning, and you've got the Dobsonian out by 10 PM — that's the Methow combo trip in a sentence.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Long, easily-walked shoreline on the state park side. Boat launch and gravel beaches. Good float-tube water if you want it.
* **Fish:** Rainbows 10–13" with regular carryover to 15"+. Brown trout (less common, larger when caught). Kokanee in summer schools — small but plentiful.
* **Hot start:** Late April through May is peak — recently-stocked + spring-fed cool water.
* **Trail:** Rex Derr Trail loops ~3.1 miles through the park; useful for sunrise walks and pre-fishing scouting.
