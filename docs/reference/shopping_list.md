# Shopping List

Compact, printable tackle reference organized by target species. Each line: **item — what it's for**.

---

## TROUT — alpine cutthroat, brook, rainbow

**Line**
- 6 lb hi-vis braid, 150 yd — mainline; thin as 1–2 lb mono, casts ultralight lures
- 4 lb fluorocarbon, small spool — primary leader
- 8 lb fluorocarbon — heavier leader for Wallace mackinaw, lowland triploids

**Spinners (1/16–1/8 oz)**
- Mepps Aglia #0, #1 (gold, silver) — different flash profile than Rooster Tails
- Panther Martin 1/16 oz (yellow/red, black/yellow) — tight vibration alternative
- Rooster Tails (yellow coachdog, brown trout) — workhorse, already owned

**Spoons**
- Kastmaster 1/12, 1/8 oz (silver, gold) — punches through wind, fishes deeper
- Little Cleo 1/8 oz (frog, nickel) — wider wobble, slow retrieves

**Bait rig (PowerBait / worms)**
- Aberdeen or octopus hooks #10–14 — small-hook rig for soft baits
- Small treble hooks #14–16 — for dough on the sliding-sinker rig
- Sliding egg sinkers, ¼–½ oz — sliding-sinker dough rig
- Small barrel swivels size 12 — stop the sliding sinker
- Tin split shot, **BB** (non-toxic, ~100/pack) — workhorse single size; stack 2–3 for more weight. Treat as single-use (tin fractures when re-opened).
- Small slip bobbers — PowerBait deeper than ~4 ft
- PowerBait chartreuse — second color beyond rainbow

**Imitation baits / soft plastics** (active fishing, no live bait required)
- PowerBait Trout Worms 3" (pink shad, bubblegum, motor oil) — most versatile add to dough
- PowerBait Mice Tails — deadly on stocked rainbows; single-hook rig
- Curl-tail grubs 1.5–2" (chartreuse, white, smoke) on 1/32–1/16 oz jigheads — works trout AND yellow perch
- Berkley Gulp! Earthworms or Maggots — softer scent alternative to PowerBait
- PowerBait Power Eggs — pairs with rainbow dough on egg+dough rig

**Bubble + fly (force multiplier on light spinning gear)**
- Clear casting bubbles — adds ½ oz casting mass, throws flies on spin rod
- Parachute Adams #14–16 — primary dry
- Elk Hair Caddis #14 — secondary dry
- Olive + black woolly buggers #10–12 — wet / streamer

**Terminal**
- Barrel swivels size 12 — bubble rigs, quick leader swaps
- Single Siwash hooks (open-eye), sizes #4–#8 — replace trebles on Rooster Tails / Mepps / Kastmasters for selective-gear water (Beckler, SF Sky tribs). Mustad 9510XS or Owner Cutting Point are the standards.
- Split-ring pliers (small) — open the split ring on a lure to swap treble → siwash cleanly

**Tools**
- Forceps / hemostats — barbless hook removal
- Line snips on a zinger — knots, retying
- Stainless steel fishing scissors — cut braid cleanly, trim soft plastics to size, snip bait

**Yellow perch crossover** — same rod/reel/line works; just add smaller hooks (#6–10) and a slip bobber + jig + worm rig.

## FLOUNDER / SOLE — Puget Sound piers & beaches

**Hooks**
- Octopus or wide-gap circle hooks #2 to 1/0 — hi/low rig staple (scale to 2/0 for bigger starry flounder)
- Long-shank baitholder #2 to 1/0 — easier removal, holds soft bait better

**Weights**
- Pyramid sinkers 2–4 oz — holds in current
- Bank sinkers 2–4 oz — mild current or sand bottom

**Rig components**
- Pre-tied dropper-loop hi/low leaders — fastest setup
- 3-way swivels size 4 — build your own hi/low
- 15–20 lb fluoro leader spool — abrasion resistance against pier structure
- Bead-chain swivels — reduce line twist on current-driven rigs

**Bait**
- Sand shrimp (frozen) — premium flatfish bait, soaks well
- Squid strips — cheap, durable, all-day soak
- Herring strips — small slivers; pair well with shrimp
- Bloodworms / sandworms — top-tier bait but pricey

**Optional**
- Sabiki rig (size 8–10) — catch fresh herring on site for cut bait
- Bait knife / scissors — strip cutting
- Latex / nitrile gloves — slime and bait handling
