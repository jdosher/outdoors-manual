# Alpine Trout Loadout (Hike-In, Shore Fishing)

Compact kit for Cascade alpine cutthroat / brook / rainbow. Target box: **KastKing HyperSeal 3500** (waterproof, double-sided, EVA foam side, includes desiccant — well-suited to backcountry condensation). Plano EDGE 3500 is the smaller minimalist alternative.

## Current Inventory (owned)
* Rod: Fenwick 6.5' Light
* Reel: Okuma 2500, spooled with **6 lb hi-vis braid (loaded as mainline)** over 6 lb mono backing
* Leader: 4 lb fluorocarbon
* Lures: Rooster Tails (assorted)
* Bait: PowerBait, rainbow
* Scent: Pro-Cure Super Gel — Trophy Trout
* Floats: small red/white snap-on bobbers (the spring style)

## Shopping List (gaps)
* **Mepps Aglia #1** — gold (different flash profile from Rooster Tails)
* **Kastmaster spoon** — 1/8 oz silver (casts farther in wind, fishes deeper)
* **8 lb fluorocarbon leader** — small spool. Required for walleye trips (Potholes) and useful for Wallace mackinaw / lowland triploids. Swap to this leader, leave the 6 lb braid mainline as-is.
* Optional second priority: Panther Martin 1/16 oz (yellow/red), casting bubble + a few small flies (Parachute Adams #14–16, olive woolly bugger).

## Tackle

* **Inline spinners (workhorse):** 1/16–1/8 oz. Carry variety in flash profile — fish refuse one style, hit another:
    - Rooster Tails (have)
    - Mepps Aglia #0–#2 (gold, silver)
    - Panther Martin (yellow/red, black/yellow)
* **Small spoons:** 1/12–1/8 oz Kastmasters or Little Cleos. 2–3 total. Heavier per size — punch through wind and reach deeper holding fish that spinners can't.
* **Bait rigging** (set-and-wait while you eat lunch):
    - Aberdeen / octopus hooks #10–14
    - Split shot assortment (BB through #4)
    - Small red/white snap-on bobbers (have) — fine for shallow PowerBait rigs. Slip bobbers are nicer if fishing deeper than ~4 ft.
    - PowerBait rainbow (have) — works on stocked rainbows and cutthroat. Chartreuse is a useful second color.
* **Bubble float + fly:** clear casting bubble (half-filled with water = ~1/2 oz) lets you throw a fly on a spinning rod. Solves both the light-lure casting problem and gives you fly presentation on cruising alpine fish.
    - Dries: Parachute Adams #14–16, Elk Hair Caddis
    - Wets / streamers: small woolly buggers (olive / black)
* **Leader:** small spool of **4 lb fluorocarbon** (have) for tippet/rebuilds. Carry **8 lb fluoro** too if heading to Wallace (mackinaw) or lowland triploid lakes.
* **Scent gel** (Pro-Cure Super Gel Trophy Trout, owned): pea-sized dab on spinners, spoons, or soft plastics. Reapply every 15–20 min. Skip on PowerBait (already scented).

## Rigs (the productive setups)

* **Sliding-sinker dough rig** (PowerBait / Power Eggs): mainline → small sliding egg sinker (¼–½ oz) → small barrel swivel → ~18" of 4 lb fluoro leader → #14–16 small treble hook molded with a marble of PowerBait. The dough floats off the bottom; the sinker pins to bottom; cruising trout find it. The "soak while you eat lunch" rig.
* **Trout worm / Mice Tail rig**: #8–10 baitholder hook with the soft bait threaded on → one small split shot 18" up the line → optional small slip bobber 3–5 ft up if fishing deeper than the lure can suspend itself. Cast and let it drift, or work it slowly back.
* **Curl-tail grub / soft plastic rig**: 1/32–1/16 oz jighead → 1.5–2" curl-tail grub → cast and slow-roll back. Active presentation; works trout AND yellow perch off the same rod.
* **Bubble + fly rig**: clear casting bubble half-filled with water → 3–6 ft of 4 lb fluoro leader → small fly (dry, wet, or streamer). Solves both the light-lure casting problem and gives you fly presentation on a spinning rod.

## Line & Casting Light Lures

For 1/16 oz lures specifically (Rooster Tails, small spinners), the line is what limits you more than rod/reel:

* **Mainline:** 6 lb hi-vis braid (yellow or green). Thin as 1–2 lb mono = casts ultralight lures dramatically better. Hi-vis is fine because the fluoro leader handles fish-side stealth.
* **Leader:** 3–4 ft of 4 lb fluoro. (8 lb for bigger-fish days.)
* **Knot — braid to fluoro:** Double Uni (easy, reliable). FG knot is technically better but overkill here.
* **Knot — fluoro to lure:** Improved Clinch or Palomar.
* **Spool loading:** put existing 6 lb mono as backing first to fill ~half the deep 2500 spool, top with braid to within 1/8" of the spool lip.

**Casting technique for 1/16 oz:**
* Pendulum / lob cast, not a snap — let the rod tip load on the back swing.
* Open the bail before the lure stalls at the apex.
* Sidearm sweep for accuracy under trees.
* Cast *with* the wind — even 5 mph headwind kills light lures.
* When wind or distance is the problem: rig the casting bubble + small spinner or fly (above).

## Leave at Home

* Anything > 1/8 oz (overkill for ~7" fish, snags bottom)
* Big crankbaits, jigs heavier than 1/16 oz, bass-sized terminal tackle
* The 3700 tray itself
