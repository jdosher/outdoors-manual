# Cooper Lake

Cooper Lake is the drive-to anchor of the Salmon La Sac fishery — a quiet 120-acre lake 3.5 miles northwest of Salmon La Sac up the Cooper River drainage, ringed by old-growth conifers and rimmed by granite peaks on the north side. The fishery is unusually diverse for an alpine-foothill lake: rainbow and eastern brook trout in the 8–10" range, plus brown trout running 1–3 lbs (occasionally larger), small kokanee, and the occasional cutthroat. **Bull trout are present and must be released** — they're ESA-listed and Cooper Lake is specifically closed to bull trout retention. The lake is open year-round, which makes it one of the few productive eastside fisheries you can hit in winter (snow road conditions permitting). A USFS boat launch sits near Owhi Campground on the north shore, but a county ordinance prohibits all motors — so you're paddling, rowing, or float-tubing.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Shore access is okay near the boat launch and around Owhi Campground; much of the rest is brushy or steep. A float tube transforms productivity here.
* **Fish:** Rainbows and brook (8–10") are the day-to-day catch. Brown trout to 3+ lbs are the trophy class — fish them deeper and slower. Kokanee small but present.
* **Bull Trout ID:** White-edged dorsal/anal/pelvic fins, no black spots on dorsal — release immediately, don't lift from water if possible.
* **Approach:** Cle Elum River Rd (Hwy 903) north to FR 46, then FR 4616 to the lake. Last 1+ miles are gravel; passable in 2WD when dry.
