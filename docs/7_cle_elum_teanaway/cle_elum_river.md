# Cle Elum River

The Cle Elum is one of the central Cascades' great fly-rod rivers — a clear, cobble-bottomed freestone that drains the Alpine Lakes Wilderness, runs through Cle Elum Lake (the reservoir), and joins the Yakima at the town of Cle Elum. **For trout, it's a catch-and-release fishery** under selective-gear rules; if you're looking for keeper fish, you'll find them in Cooper Lake or Cle Elum Lake's kokanee, not here. The river holds all four major salmonid species — wild rainbow, native cutthroat, ESA-listed bull trout, and kokanee — plus mountain whitefish that are open to retention under statewide rules and are actually a legitimate eat in winter. The lower reach (mouth to Cle Elum Dam) is open year-round with selective gear, with a special winter window allowing whitefish gear. The upper reach above the lake (up to Hyas Lake outlet) is open Saturday before Memorial Day through October 31, also selective gear.

## Regulations (verbatim from WDFW pamphlet)

### Lower reach — from mouth to Cle Elum Dam
> **All species:** It is permissible to fish up to the base of Cle Elum Dam.
> **Mar. 1 – Nov. 30:** Selective gear rules.
> **Dec. 1 – Last Day of Feb.:** Selective gear rules except whitefish gear may be used.
> **Trout (year-round):** Statewide min. size/daily limit. **Except: Release cutthroat trout and rainbow trout.**
> **Other game fish (year-round):** Statewide min. size/daily limit.

### Upper reach — above Cle Elum Lake to outlet of Hyas Lake
> **All species:** Selective gear rules.
> **All game fish:** Sat. before Memorial Day – Oct. 31. Statewide min. size/daily limit.

### What "Selective Gear Rules" actually means
* **Single-point barbless hooks only**
* **No bait** of any kind (no PowerBait, Mice Tails, Trout Worms, Power Eggs, sand shrimp, salmon eggs, worms)
* Artificial flies and lures only

### Bull Trout
* Present throughout the Cle Elum drainage; **catch-and-release statewide, ESA-listed**
* ID: white-edged dorsal/anal/pelvic fins, no black spots on dorsal fin
* When in doubt → release

## Why fish a C&R river?
* Wild fish quality — Cle Elum cutthroat and rainbow are educated, beautiful, and well-conditioned
* Drift presentation practice that pays off elsewhere
* The river itself is a gorgeous place to spend a half-day
* **Mountain whitefish are retainable** and are actually excellent smoked — don't sleep on this in winter

## Tackle
✅ **Will work:**
* **Inline spinners** (Rooster Tails, Mepps Aglia #0–#1, Panther Martin) with trebles crushed barbless OR replaced with single siwash hooks
* **Small spoons** (Kastmaster 1/12–1/8 oz, Little Cleo) — same hook conversion
* **Bubble + fly rig** with small dries (Parachute Adams, Elk Hair Caddis) or wets (soft hackles, small streamers, small nymphs)
* **Existing Fenwick 6.5' Light + Okuma 2500 + 4 lb fluoro leader** = right setup

❌ **Leave in the car:**
* All scented baits — PowerBait, Power Eggs, Mice Tails, Trout Worms
* Gulp! / Berkley scented soft plastics
* Sand shrimp, salmon eggs, worms
* Treble hooks unless converted to single barbless
