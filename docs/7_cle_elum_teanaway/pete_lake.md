# Pete Lake

Pete Lake is the quintessential easy-hike-in alpine trout lake — 4 miles of nearly-flat trail following the Cooper River through old-growth Douglas fir and western hemlock to a long lake sitting in the shadow of Big Summit Chief and Chimney Rock. The trail's gentle grade makes it accessible for families and first-time backpackers; pack animals and equestrians share it. The fishery is **eastern brook trout** of varying sizes — the shoreline is shallow over much of the lake, which makes shore casting more work than usual, but the brookies are willing and the place is genuinely beautiful. Backcountry campsites at the lake fill up on summer weekends; midweek and shoulder seasons are golden. Early-season water crossings on the trail can be tricky for kids.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Long shoreline but much of it is shallow with submerged structure. Best shore-casting is from the inlet and outlet ends and at a few small clearings around the perimeter; bring polarized sunglasses to sight-fish cruising brookies. A packable float tube would punch above its weight here.
* **Fish:** Eastern brook trout, varying sizes — typical alpine fish are 8–12" but bigger holdovers exist. Stocked over the years; now reproducing.
* **Trail Approach:** From Cooper Lake trailhead area (off FR 46 then FR 4616), 4 miles one-way, ~400 ft gain. Gentle. Equestrian and pack-stock traffic on busy days.
* **Continuing past Pete:** Spectacle Lake is another 2.5 miles further up; the PCT crosses just past Pete Lake. A bigger day or overnight option.
