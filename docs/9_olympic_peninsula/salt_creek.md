# Salt Creek Recreation Area

Salt Creek Recreation Area is a Clallam County park on the Strait of Juan de Fuca, about 16 miles west of Port Angeles near Joyce. The campground sits on a grassy bluff with big-water views north to Vancouver Island, and the fishing is genuinely two-in-one: **saltwater shore casting** on the Strait (Marine Area 6) plus a small **coastal stream** — Salt Creek itself — running through the park to the beach at Crescent Bay. No boat required; everything here is worked from the beach and rocks.

The catch: the entire park shoreline is a protected tidepool sanctuary. That does **not** shut down rod-and-reel fishing (see the regulations note below), but it does mean **do not remove any tidepool life, shells, rocks, or driftwood** — and clams/mussels/oysters are **closed year-round**.

## The Tidepool Sanctuary — What It Does and Doesn't Ban

The whole Salt Creek shoreline (and especially **Tongue Point**, some of the best tidepool habitat in the state) is a no-take marine protected area under **Clallam County Code 23.03.140(6)**. The ordinance reads:

> *"It is unlawful for any person or persons to remove driftwood, shells, rocks, or any form of marine life... Marine life is designated as sea stars, anemones, etc. **This chapter does not apply to fish caught by sport fishing during season with legal limits.**"*

So, in plain terms:

* ✅ **Rod-and-reel fishing is allowed**, and you may keep legal fish (salmon, surfperch, flounder, etc.) taken in season within legal limits and with a license.
* ❌ **Do not remove** sea stars, anemones, other tidepool invertebrates, shells, rocks, or driftwood — look, photograph, touch, but leave it.
* ❌ **Shellfish (clams, mussels, oysters) are CLOSED year-round** here.
* ⚠️ **Crab** is not clearly carved out by the ordinance the way finfish is, and Salt Creek has no pier/dock — treat Dungeness as a boat fishery elsewhere in MA 6, not a shore option here.

## Prime Shore Spots

* **Crescent Bay beach (west of Tongue Point)** — the main shore-casting beach, and it's the **private half-mile beach fronting Crescent Beach & RV Park** — i.e. right out the door of our campsite (see Camping/Logistics). This is the salmon and surfperch water, and it sits **west of the Freshwater Bay closure** (see salmon note). Best on tide changes.
* **Tongue Point rocks** — dramatic structure and tidepools. Fishy-looking, but **rockfish are closed** and lingcod is out of season in summer; mind the tide and slick rock. Primarily a look-don't-take tidepool spot.
* **Beach gravel along the bluff** — surfperch and flounder/sole on a falling tide.
* **Salt Creek mouth / lagoon** — sea-run cutthroat stage near the estuary; the creek itself is freshwater (see below). Respect the freshwater salmon closure.

## Saltwater — Species & Regulations (2026–27, Marine Area 6)

**Salt Creek is well west of Ediz Hook, in the open portion of MA 6. Always confirm each species at the [WDFW MA 6 page](https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/locations/marine-areas/east-juan-de-fuca-strait) or the current pamphlet before fishing — marine seasons are re-set every year.**

### Salmon — the headline shore fishery
* **Jul 1 – Aug 15:** Open. **Hatchery Chinook selective** (Chinook min. size 22", adipose-clipped only); other salmon no min. size. **Daily limit 2.**
* **Aug 16 – Sept 24 and Sept 25 – Oct 15:** Open, **daily limit 2, release Chinook** — this is the coho window.
* **Coho** is the realistic shore target from the beach, peaking **late August into September.**
* ⚠️ **Freshwater Bay closure:** the water *south of a line from Angeles Point west to Observatory Point (Bachelor Rock)* — i.e. the bay **east of Tongue Point** — is closed to **all species July 1 – Oct 31.** Fish the **Crescent Bay side, west of Tongue Point**, which is outside this closure. Confirm the line before casting.
* **Tactics:** Buzz Bombs, Pt. Wilson Darts, casting spoons (1/2–3/4 oz Kastmaster/Krocodile) on the incoming tide; first and last hour of light are best.
* **Requires:** WA saltwater license **plus a salmon Catch Record Card.**

### Surfperch — the reliable summer keeper
* **Open year-round. No min. size. Daily limit 10** (shiner perch 15).
* **Tactics:** Sand shrimp, mussel, or bloodworm on a high-low rig off the beach gravel; best on a falling tide.

### Flounder / Sole (flatfish)
* **"Other Food Fish" — daily limit 2 of each species.** Open. Taken on shrimp/worm rigs off the sandy stretches.

### Sea-run Cutthroat (saltwater)
* Present along the beaches and near the creek mouth; light tackle, small spoons/streamers or sand shrimp. Game-fish rules apply — **verify current saltwater cutthroat rules** (typically release-oriented near stream mouths).

### Lingcod — **CLOSED this weekend**
* **Season: May 1 – June 15 only** (min. 26", max. 36", 1/day). Not available in summer.

### Rockfish — **CLOSED**
* **Closed to fishing and retention** throughout MA 6. If one comes up incidentally, release it with a **descending device**.

## Freshwater — Salt Creek (the stream)

Salt Creek is a small coastal stream, not a destination trout fishery, but it runs through the park and holds **coastal (resident) cutthroat**, with sea-run cutthroat staging near the mouth in season.

* **Season:** WA statewide stream season — **open the Saturday before Memorial Day through Oct. 31** (so open this weekend).
* **Salmon & steelhead:** freshwater is **closed to salmon** by default; **bull trout/Dolly Varden closed.** No special rule was found opening Salt Creek to salmon — treat it as **trout only.**
* **Trout:** No Salt Creek–specific special rule was located. Coastal cutthroat streams commonly require **selective gear (single barbless, no bait)** and **release of wild trout.** Safest assumption: **catch-and-release, selective gear.** ⚠️ **Verify the special-rules listing for "Salt Creek (Clallam)" in the current pamphlet before keeping anything.**
* **License:** WA freshwater (or combo) license.

## Camping — Crescent Beach & RV Park (our basecamp)

We're staying at the **private** **Crescent Beach & RV Park** on Crescent Bay — **not** the county park next door. It fronts its own **half-mile private sandy beach**, which is the prime shore-casting/salmon/surfperch water (west of Tongue Point, outside the Freshwater Bay closure), so the good beach is right at the campsite.

* **Sites:** tent, partial & full-hookup RV, plus 2 cabins; front-row full-hookup sites have the clearest water/Strait views (Vancouver Island on clear days).
* **Amenities:** picnic table + firepit per site, Wi-Fi, laundry, restrooms, hot showers, small store.
* **Reserve:** private business, book direct — [crescentbeachrv.com](https://www.crescentbeachrv.com/). Summer weekends fill; reserve ahead.
* **Location:** ~0.5 mi **west** of Salt Creek Recreation Area — easy walk east to the county park's tidepools (Tongue Point), day-use, and WWII gun-battery ruins.
