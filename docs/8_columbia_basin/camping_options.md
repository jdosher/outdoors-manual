# Columbia Basin Camping for a 26' Trailer

The Columbia Basin spreads camping across a few distinct buckets — BLM dispersed, state-park developed, and private RV resorts. For a 3–5 day fall quail hunt with a 26' trailer, here are the real options.

## Best Picks (ranked for the use case)

### 1. Potholes State Park (developed, hookups)
* **Location:** South of Moses Lake, on Potholes Reservoir
* **Closest to:** Desert Wildlife Area (~20 min)
* **Sites:** 60+ utility sites with full or partial hookups; standard tent sites; restrooms with showers
* **Trailer fit:** Easy for 26'
* **Season:** Open year-round (verify; some WA state parks have winter closures)
* **Pros:** Hot showers, dump station, water, easy access to Desert WA hunting
* **Cons:** Reservable system (peak summer is hard; fall is usually open mid-week)
* **The right call if:** comfort/hookups matter more than dispersed feel

### 2. Frenchman Coulee dispersed (BLM, near Vantage)
* **Location:** Off I-90 just east of the Columbia River crossing at Vantage
* **Closest to:** Quincy Lakes WA (~15–20 min)
* **Sites:** Multiple established dispersed sites for tents and RVs
* **Trailer fit:** 26' workable at established pullouts; varies by exact spot
* **Season:** Open year-round
* **Pros:** Free, dispersed feel, scenic basalt walls, climbing/hiking nearby
* **Cons:** Primitive (no toilets at most spots, no water, no trash), can get windy
* **The right call if:** you're self-contained and want the dispersed BLM experience near Quincy

### 3. Saddle Mountains BLM dispersed (north of Mattawa)
* **Location:** West end of the Saddle Mountains, accessed via "R" road from Mattawa
* **Closest to:** Desert WA southern access, Wahluke Slope hunting
* **Sites:** Dispersed throughout; established sites at established pullouts
* **Trailer fit:** Roads are gravel; 26' fine with caution
* **Season:** Open year-round
* **Pros:** Big, remote, low-pressure, Columbia River views
* **Cons:** Roads can wash out; truly primitive (bring everything)
* **The right call if:** you want the most remote/private base camp

### 4. Sun Lakes-Dry Falls State Park (developed, hookups)
* **Location:** North of Coulee City
* **Closest to:** Quincy Lakes WA (~45 min — further than Frenchman Coulee), Desert WA (~1 hr)
* **Sites:** Full hookups, tent sites, cabins
* **Trailer fit:** Easy for 26'
* **Pros:** Beautiful setting (Dry Falls), full amenities, fishing on the lakes
* **Cons:** Drive time to hunting areas adds up
* **The right call if:** combining hunting with fishing / scenery / family

## Honest Trip Plan Recommendations

**Mid-week, 3–5 days, fall quail focus, 26' trailer:**

* **Hookup-focused:** Potholes SP → Desert WA hunting. ~20 min drive from camp to hunt each day. Hot shower at night.
* **Dispersed-focused:** Frenchman Coulee or Saddle Mtns → both Quincy walk-in and Desert WA drive-in within reach. Self-contained.
* **Two-base ambitious:** 2 nights at Frenchman Coulee (Quincy walk-in hunts) + 2 nights at Potholes SP or Saddle Mtns (Desert WA hunts). Moves the trailer once but doubles your unit exposure.

## What's NOT a Good Fit

* **Inside Quincy Lakes WA itself** — main access road closed Oct 1 – Mar 1, can't drive trailer in
* **Inside Desert WA** — Reclamation MOU restrictions on camping in the unit itself; verify before assuming
* **Wenas/Umtanum-style dispersed** — that's a different region with different access; don't confuse the two
