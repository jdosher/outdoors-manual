# Columbia Basin Camping for a 26' Trailer with Hookups

For fall hunts (Oct–Dec), **hookups are the right call** — temperatures drop steeply at dusk, electric heat is welcome, and there's no penalty for being civilized. All recommended options below are open during the full fall quail season unless noted.

## Geographic Reality First

The two huntable areas are on **opposite sides of the Columbia Basin**:

| Hunt Area | Location | Closest hookup town | Drive between areas |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Desert Wildlife Area** | West of Moses Lake | Moses Lake (Potholes SP, etc.) | — |
| **Quincy Lakes WA Unit** (part of Columbia Basin WA) | West of George / near Vantage | Vantage / Quincy | **~50 min between areas** |

**Pick one primary hunt area and camp near it. Don't try to base near both.** Quincy Lakes is walk-in only during quail season (Oct 1 – Mar 1 road closure inside the unit), and Desert WA is the bigger / more diverse / vehicle-accessible option — making **Desert WA the strong default primary**.

## Primary Recommendation: Desert WA hunt + Moses Lake-area hookup camp

### Potholes State Park (state park, full hookups) — the headline pick
* **Location:** South of Moses Lake on Potholes Reservoir
* **Distance to Desert WA:** ~20 min ✓
* **Distance to Quincy Lakes Unit:** ~50 min
* **Sites:** **60 full-hookup sites**, accommodates RVs up to 50' (your 26' is easy)
* **Season:** Open year-round
* **Amenities:** Dump station, hot showers, restrooms
* **Reservations:** Required — [Washington State Parks reservation system](https://washington.goingtocamp.com/)
* **Pros:** State park reliability, closest hookup base to Desert WA, full amenities
* **Cons:** Reservation system; book early. Mid-week fall is your best availability window.

### Mar Don Resort (private, full hookups, on Potholes Reservoir)
* **Location:** Adjacent to Potholes SP, ~16 mi SW of Moses Lake
* **Distance to Desert WA:** ~20 min ✓
* **Sites:** Full hookups, tent sites, monthly options
* **Season:** Year-round
* **Amenities:** **Marina, restaurant & bar, swimming beach** — on-site dining
* **Pros:** Restaurant on-site (no need to cook every night), good Potholes SP backup
* **Cons:** Pricier; resort vibe over hunting-camp vibe

### Suncrest RV Park (private, full hookups, in Moses Lake town)
* **Location:** 303 Hansen Rd, Moses Lake
* **Distance to Desert WA:** ~25 min
* **Sites:** 87 full hookup sites
* **Season:** Year-round
* **Pros:** Easy resupply (restaurants, grocery, fuel within minutes); high availability
* **Cons:** Town setting, no hunting-area feel

### Cascade Campground (city park, partial hookups, budget)
* **Location:** Moses Lake city park
* **Distance to Desert WA:** ~25 min
* **Sites:** Partial hookups (typically 30 amp electric + water; dump station)
* **Reservations:** Phone 509-764-3805, M–F 8a–5p
* **Pros:** Cheapest hookup option, in-town convenience
* **Cons:** Partial only

## Secondary Option: Quincy Lakes Unit + Vantage/Quincy-area camp

Only do this if you specifically want to hunt Quincy Lakes Unit. Be aware Quincy Lakes is walk-in only during quail season.

### Crescent Bar Recreation Area / Crescent Bar RV Campground (private, full hookups)
* **Location:** 9252 Crescent Bar Rd NW, Quincy — on the Columbia River
* **Distance to Quincy Lakes WA:** ~15 min
* **Sites:** 55 RV sites with hookups
* **Season:** Verify with the operator (the Thousand Trails *Crescent Bar RV Resort* is separate and closes after September; the **Crescent Bar Recreation Area** is the year-round-ish option — call to confirm fall availability)

### Vantage Riverstone Resort (private, full hookups)
* **Location:** Vantage, WA
* **Distance to Quincy Lakes WA:** ~15–20 min
* **Sites:** Full hookups, 30/50 amp, water/electric/sewer; grassy/gravel sites with Columbia River views
* **Rates:** $36–55/night
* **Season:** Verify year-round status by phone

### Sage Creek Campground (private, partial-to-full)
* **Location:** Quincy area, across the Columbia River
* **Three tiers:** full hookups, partial, no-hookup-with-generator-allowed

### ⚠ DO NOT base around these for fall hunt
* **Wanapum State Park** — **CLOSES Oct 24** for winter. Useful only Oct 4–24 of quail season.
* **Thousand Trails Crescent Bar RV Resort** — closes after September. Wrong season entirely.

## Middle-Ground Option (if you really want to hit both areas)

### Ephrata RV Park (private, full hookups, central Grant Co.)
* **Location:** Ephrata, central Grant County
* **Distance to Quincy Lakes WA:** ~20–25 min
* **Distance to Desert WA:** ~30 min
* **Sites:** Big-rig friendly 30/50 amp full hookup pull-throughs
* **Season:** Year-round
* **Pros:** Closest single base to BOTH areas; pull-through ease for a 26' rig
* **Cons:** Not the closest to either area individually; town setting

## Decision Rule

* **Desert WA focus (primary recommendation) + hookup priority** → **Potholes SP** (reserve early), Mar Don Resort backup
* **Quincy Lakes focus + hookups** → **Vantage Riverstone Resort** or **Crescent Bar Recreation Area**
* **Want to hunt both areas from one camp** → **Ephrata RV Park** (longer drives but central)
* **Best resupply / restaurants** → **Suncrest RV** or **Mar Don Resort**
* **Cheapest hookup** → **Cascade Campground**

## Booking Note

Reservable state parks (Potholes SP) need advance booking even for fall. **Mid-week is your friend.** Call private RV parks directly — they typically have more availability and aren't gamed by booking bots like the state-park system.
