# Hidden Lake

Hidden Lake is the strenuous, scenic option — a hike-first, fish-second outing for the days you want the views as much as the catch. The lake sits at 5,733 ft in a granite cirque east of the main summit, with the historic Hidden Lake Lookout (built 1931, 6,850 ft) perched on the ridge above. The trail climbs 3,300 ft over 4.5 miles, passing through forest, meadows, and finally stark alpine granite well above treeline. Snow lingers late here; the lake stays frozen into late June or early July most years, and snowfields on the trail can persist longer. Reports mention trout in the water but the species and density aren't well-documented — this is genuinely exploratory territory, and field notes you collect here would actually fill a gap.

**Trailhead:** From Marblemount on Hwy 20, take Cascade River Rd ~10 mi to FR 1540, then 4.5 mi rough road to the trailhead. Road is rough enough that high clearance is recommended.

## Scouting Report
* **Bank Conditions:** Granite shoreline, limited brush. The lake often stays ice-covered into late June or early July — fish accordingly.
* **Fish:** Reports mention trout but the species and density are not well-documented. Treat as exploratory.
* **The Lookout:** First-come/first-serve overnight stays; very popular, often full.
* **Difficulty:** Steep, exposed sections high up. Snowfields can persist into July — bring traction.
