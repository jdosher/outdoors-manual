# North Cascades / Highway 20 Corridor Overview

* **Primary Passes:** Northwest Forest Pass (USFS trailheads), no fee inside North Cascades NP proper (but a WA fishing license is still required for keeping fish).
* **Key Targets:** Westslope Cutthroat dominate the alpine lakes — many were stocked decades ago and now self-sustain. Some rainbow and brook in select waters.
* **Seasonality — the two-stage gate:**
    1. **Hwy 20 reopens** typically late April or May (earliest on record: 3/10/2005; latest: 6/14/1974; recent norm has been May). Closes between mileposts 130 and 177 from ~Thanksgiving on.
    2. **Lake ice-out lags the road by weeks.** Alpine lakes in the 4,800–5,000+ ft band (Rainy, Cutthroat, Wing, Ann, etc.) typically clear ice **late June through July**, depending on snowpack year. Trails at this elevation can hold snow well into June even after the highway is open.
* **Practical fishing window:** **Mid-July through September** is the safe bet for these lakes. May/June trips are scenic but you may be looking at ice + snow-bound trails. Higher cirques (Hidden Lake at 5,733 ft) skew later — often not fishable until July.
* **Land Mosaic:** Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie NF (west), North Cascades NP / Ross Lake NRA (middle), Okanogan-Wenatchee NF (east). Pass and rule details vary by which side of the boundary you're on — verify per-trailhead.
* **Camping in NP:** Backcountry camping inside North Cascades NP requires a permit from the Marblemount ranger station; bear canisters required in many zones.
