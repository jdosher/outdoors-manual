# Rainy Lake

Rainy is a 53-acre alpine lake reached by a paved, wheelchair-accessible trail off Hwy 20 at Rainy Pass — the rare Cascade lake you can visit in dress shoes. Steep cliffs rim the south shore and feed the lake with hanging-glacier meltwater and cascading waterfalls dropping hundreds of feet off the rock. The water is glacially clear; on calm days you can see cutthroat cruising 20 ft out from the observation deck. The deck itself is the main and possibly only easy bank-access point — WDFW explicitly recommends bringing a float tube to fish the rest of the lake, where the action is. Foot traffic is heavy in midsummer because the trail is so accessible, so dawn and the shoulder season are when this lake is best. The cutthroat run a respectable 10–11", noticeably larger than most Hwy 20 alpine fish — worth the trip even just from the deck.

## Scouting Report
* **Shore Access Caveat:** Per WDFW, you really want floatation here — *"if you bring floatation you can not only get off by yourself, you can also catch some of the abundant Westslope Cutthroat."* The paved trail ends at an observation deck on the north shore; that's the main / possibly only easy bank access. Float tube transforms this lake.
* **Fish:** Abundant Westslope Cutthroat averaging **10–11"** — genuinely good size for an alpine lake.
* **Pressure:** Very high (tourist-accessible, wheelchair-paved). Best at dawn / late evening or shoulder season.
* **Approach:** Trailhead at Rainy Pass (milepost 158); large parking lot shared with Maple Pass Loop trailhead — often full midday in summer.
* **Snow Caveat:** At 4,855 ft, the trail can hold snow well into June even after Hwy 20 reopens — a bootpack usually exists. The lake itself often stays partially iced. See [Area Overview](Overview.md) for the corridor-wide ice-out pattern.
